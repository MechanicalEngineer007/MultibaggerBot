Enterprise-grade algorithmic **Multibagger Stock Discovery & Machine Learning Screener** engineered specifically for the Indian Stock Market (NSE & BSE), featuring both a native **Android Mobile Application** (Jetpack Compose) and a full-featured **Desktop Screener Suite** (Python / CLI / Standalone Dashboard).

Unlike short-term swing trading, **Multibagger Bot** is engineered to discover high-conviction medium-to-long term compounders using a confluence of:
1. **Stan Weinstein Stage-2 Structural Markup** & **Mark Minervini 8-Point Trend Template**
2. **Fundamental Moat & Solvency Analysis** (High PAT/Sales CAGR, ROCE > 20%, Low Debt / Net Cash, PEG GARP)
3. **Machine Learning Ensemble** (Gradient Boosted models calibrated on historical 2x cycles)
4. **Institutional Delivery Volume Footprint** (NSE Delivery % & Pocket Pivots)
5. **Multi-Model Quantitative Target Consensus** (2-Stage DCF, GBM, Monte Carlo, ARIMA, and LSTM)

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🎯 Multibagger Radar
- Curated scan of emerging leaders from **Nifty Midcap 150 & Smallcap 250** (e.g., *MCX, BSE, SYRMA, SOLARINDS, BHEL, MANKIND, NETWEB, DIXON, KAYNES, POLYCAB*).
- **Custom Search**: Instant diagnosis of any NSE/BSE stock ticker.
- **Convenient Filters**:
  - ⭐ *AAA Prime (Score 80+)*
  - 🎯 *1-Year Target > 35%*
  - 🚀 *2-Year 2x+ Multibaggers (>100% Projected Upside)*

### 2. 📈 Stage-2 Technical Engine
- Minervini 8-Point Trend Template verification (50-DMA > 150-DMA > 200-DMA).
- Upward 200-DMA slope confirmation over 1+ months.
- ATH proximity filter (within 20–25% of 52-Week High) and >30% above 52-Week Low.
- Up/Down volume accumulation ratio and delivery volume surge analysis.

### 3. 💵 DCF & Quantitative Target Projections
- **2-Stage Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)**: Calculates 1-Year and 5-Year intrinsic values and Margin of Safety.
- **Quantitative Consensus Engine**: Aggregates Geometric Brownian Motion (GBM), Monte Carlo simulations, ARIMA time series, and Deep Learning targets into a consensus projection.
- **Trailing Cushion Floor**: 200-DMA protective buffer (~15–18% maximum structured risk).

### 4. 💰 Wealth Simulator
- Interactive capital allocation simulator (e.g., ₹1,00,000, ₹5,00,000).
- Simulates 1-Year and 2-Year portfolio value based on compound growth projections.
- Equal-weight portfolio allocation calculator with exact share counts.

### 5. ⚡ 1-Click Broker Integration
- **Zerodha Kite Buy**: Copies ticker and CMP to clipboard, launching Kite with order parameters ready.
- **Groww**: One-tap direct stock lookup and execution.
